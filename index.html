        How it was made

        One script drives everything

        The words on screen, the timings, the subtitles, the claims and the sound cues all live in content/script.json. The film, the subtitle files, this page and the fact check are generated from it, so they cannot drift apart.

        Animated in code, in the brand kit

        Every scene is drawn in HTML, SVG and canvas with Readex Pro, the official palette and the official logo files from the Redbelly brand kit, untouched. Each frame is a pure function of time, captured losslessly in headless Chromium and encoded once.

        Narration and original sound

        A synthetic voice reads the subtitles: Chatterbox by Resemble AI, MIT licence, its built-in voice, generated by audio/voice.py. Speech recognition confirms every line word for word, in each take and in the finished films. The score and every effect are synthesised from sine waves, saw waves and filtered noise by audio/compose.py, and the music dips under the voice.

        Facts you can check

        Each claim carries word-for-word quotes from public pages. npm run check-sources re-reads the live pages and confirms every quote is still there.
