## How the logos are used

The Redbelly brand guidelines ask for the logo to keep its proportions and clear space, to stay legible against its background, and to use only the versions supplied in the brand pack. The film follows that:

- The logo files are byte-for-byte the files in the brand pack. `tools/verify.mjs` re-hashes them against `official-hashes.json`.
- The logo reveal draws a hexagon over the isotype's outline and then shows the official artwork with a clip wipe. The finished lockup is the untouched official file at its own proportions.
- Logos only appear on the dark brand slate (#283A46 to #142530), with the negative versions made for dark backgrounds.

## How the narration is made

- The voice reads the film's own subtitles from `content/script.json`, which are fact-checked line by line. Where a subtitle is written differently from how it is said, the caption carries a `say` field ("CSIRO" is said letter by letter, "redbelly.network" as "Redbelly dot network").
- Each line is recorded at least twice. Every take is transcribed by speech recognition (Whisper) and the take that says every word and fits the time its subtitle is on screen is kept.
- The finished films are transcribed again (`audio/check_mix.py`) to confirm every line is understood over the music. `tools/verify.mjs` checks both results.
- Chatterbox adds an inaudible watermark to its output that identifies the audio as synthetic speech. It is left in.
